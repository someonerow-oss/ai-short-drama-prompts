# 题材模板：战神归来

## 一、核心爽点
**身份落差 + 当众亮牌**。观众要的是"被看不起 → 揭底 → 全场跪下"的完整链条。爽点强度 = 前面被踩的深度。

## 二、人物配置

| 角色 | 八槽位 | 作用 |
|---|---|---|
| 男主 | 30 岁，轮廓冷硬，单眼皮锐利，寸头/短黑发，188cm 宽肩，外卖服 → 定制黑西装，左手虎口有旧疤 | 隐藏身份的战神 |
| 女主 | 25 岁，女儿/妻子，温婉坚韧，素色连衣裙 | 男主唯一的软肋 |
| 反派（岳家/亲戚）| 50 岁中年男，发福，金丝眼镜，名牌西装 | 势利眼，制造羞辱 |
| 反派打手 | 30 岁壮硕，黑色皮衣 | 前期压制、后期被打脸 |
| 老部下 | 40 岁，军装/西装，肃穆 | 亮牌的执行者 |

## 六、提示词样例

**开篇羞辱（high angle 压主角）**

```
cinematic film still, photorealistic, anamorphic lens, film grain, high contrast,
medium shot, high angle, static, 50mm,
hard top light, cool 5600K, harsh shadows, dark banquet hall,
young Chinese man in blue delivery uniform being pointed at by an overweight man in luxury suit,
family members laughing around a round dinner table, man lowering his head,
humiliation, suppressed anger, cold blue tones
```

**亮牌（低角度 + 轮廓光）**

```
cinematic film still, photorealistic, anamorphic lens, film grain, teal and orange grading,
full shot, low angle, slow dolly in, 24mm,
hard key light, strong rim light, dark background, volumetric haze,
row of men in black suits standing at attention, one senior officer kneeling on one knee,
Chinese man in black tailored suit standing in the center, backlit silhouette,
overwhelming authority, epic scale, hero framing
```

**反跪下（对照组）**

```
cinematic film still, film grain, high contrast,
medium close-up, eye-level, static, 85mm, shallow depth of field,
cool daylight, hard side light,
overweight Chinese man in luxury suit on his knees, sweat on forehead, hands trembling,
marble floor, blurred crowd behind,
defeat and fear, cold satisfaction
```

## 七、运镜要点

- **前期（被压）**：`high angle` + `static` + 长焦压缩 → 空间变窄、人变小。
- **反转瞬间**：从 `high angle` 一剪到 `low angle` + `slow dolly in` → 观众生理上感到"翻身"。
- **爽点段**：用 `orbit shot` 环绕男主一圈，配合 `rim light` + `dark background`。这是整个题材最值钱的一个镜头，不要省。

## 八、禁忌

- ❌ 前期忍让不超过 2 集。第 3 集必须亮牌，否则观众流失。
- ❌ 亮牌靠"打电话给某个神秘人"。要**当面**、**当众**、**有视觉证据**（军装、跪礼、黑车队）。
- ❌ 反派一次性全灭。留 1 个更大的幕后，接续第 2 季。
- ❌ 男主对女主也高高在上。他对全世界冷，只对她软——这是角色成立的唯一逻辑。
